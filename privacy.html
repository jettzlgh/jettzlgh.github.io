Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what data jettz_clips (the "app") handles and where it goes. jettz_clips is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It has no server and no user accounts of its own: we, the developers of jettz_clips, do not receive, store or have access to your videos, your TikTok account data or anything else you do in the app.

Data stored on your computer

  • The videos you add, the clips the app renders, their transcripts, captions and settings.
  • The app settings you enter, including the TikTok app keys you configure.
  • When you connect TikTok: your TikTok account's open ID, display name, username and avatar, and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues for the permissions you granted.
  • Information about your posts that you type in or that TikTok returns (publishing status, post link, and the views, likes and comments you enter).

All of it stays in the app's folder on your computer. You can delete it at any time by deleting that folder.

Data shared with third parties, at your request

  • TikTok. When you connect your account, you sign in on TikTok's own page and choose which permissions to grant. When you publish a clip, the app sends the video file, the caption and the settings you chose to TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok's handling of this data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude). To choose and cut moments and to write captions, the app sends transcripts, still frames from the video and clip details to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, using your own Claude account or API key. This is governed by your agreement with Anthropic. Your TikTok tokens and account data are never sent to Claude.
  • Twitch and YouTube. If you add a video by link, the app downloads it from that platform.

We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking, in the app or on this website.

TikTok data

jettz_clips only uses TikTok data to show which account is connected, to check the posting options TikTok allows for it, and to publish and follow the status of the clips you choose. It does not read your other videos, followers or messages. To stop access, use "Disconnect" in the app, which revokes the token, or remove jettz_clips from the connected apps in your TikTok settings, then delete the app's folder to remove the stored data.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

jettz_clips is not directed at children and is meant for users who meet TikTok's minimum age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
