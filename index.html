Long streams in, short clips out.

aiclip is a desktop app for streamers and clip editors. It finds the best moments in a stream recording, cuts them into vertical clips with the facecam on top, the gameplay below and animated captions, and helps you publish them to TikTok. It runs on your own computer (macOS or Windows): your videos never go to an aiclip server, because there isn't one.

How it works

  1. Add a video

    Pick a stream recording on your computer, or a Twitch or YouTube video from a channel you follow.

  2. Find the moments

    The speech is transcribed on your computer. An AI model reads the transcript and looks at frames to choose and cut the moments worth posting.

  3. Edit the clips

    Each clip is rendered in 9:16 with captions and a hook. You can zoom, reframe, change the hook or the caption, or retouch a clip with a comment.

  4. Publish

    Connect your TikTok account and publish a clip now or at a time you choose, with the caption, visibility and interaction settings you pick.

Features

Vertical layout

The facecam is detected and placed above the gameplay, with zoom and position adjustable per clip.

Animated captions

Word-by-word captions from a local transcription, plus an on-screen hook for the first seconds.

Captions and hashtags

A suggested post caption and hashtags for each clip, which you can edit before posting.

Posting queue

Clips are ranked so you know what to post first; publishing slots spread posts over the day.

Results

You can type in each post's views, likes and comments to see what works and improve the next selections.

Local first

Videos, clips, settings and account tokens are stored only on your computer.

TikTok integration

aiclip uses TikTok Login Kit to connect your account and the Content Posting API to publish the clips you choose. Nothing is ever posted without your action: you pick the clip, the caption, who can see the video, and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, then confirm. Before publishing, aiclip shows your TikTok account name and the visibility options TikTok allows for it, and lets you disclose branded or promotional content.

PermissionWhy aiclip asks for it
user.info.basicShow which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar), so you know where a clip will be posted.
video.uploadSend a clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft, to finish and publish it in the TikTok app.
video.publishPublish a clip directly to your profile with the caption and settings you chose, now or at the time you scheduled.

You can disconnect your account in aiclip at any time, which also revokes its access, or remove aiclip from the apps connected to your TikTok account in TikTok's settings.
